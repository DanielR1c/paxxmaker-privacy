# Privacy Policy – PaxxMaker U1

**Last updated:** May 2026

## Overview
PaxxMaker U1 is a hobby project developed and maintained by a single developer in their spare time. This privacy policy explains how the app handles your data.

## Data Collection
PaxxMaker U1 does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers.

All communication happens **directly and exclusively** between your iPhone/Apple Watch and your 3D printer on your local network (or via OctoEverywhere).

## Data Stored Locally on Your Device
The following data is stored locally on your device only:
- Printer connection details (IP address, name, color theme)
- Custom GCode macros
- App preferences and layout settings
- NFC filament tag data (read/written locally)

This data never leaves your device except when using OctoEverywhere for remote access, in which case their own privacy policy applies.

## Third-Party Services
- **OctoEverywhere** (optional remote access): [https://octoeverywhere.com/privacy](https://octoeverywhere.com/privacy)

## Camera Stream
The MJPEG camera stream is loaded directly from your printer. No video data is stored or transmitted elsewhere.

## Children's Privacy
This app is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from children.
